DAX Formulas & Data Modeling Architecture

Inspect calculation measures and the underlying Star Schema data model

[Total Sales INR] Core Financial 
Total Sales INR = 
SUM ( 'Fact_Retail_Orders'[Gross_Sales_Amount] )

Aggregates top-line gross invoiced sales revenue in Indian Rupees across the active filter context.

[Net Realized Profit] Profitability 
Net Realized Profit = 
SUM ( 'Fact_Retail_Orders'[Net_Profit_Amount] )

Calculates realized profit after deducting wholesale procurement cost, logistics, and discounts.

[Blended Profit Margin %] Efficiency 
Blended Profit Margin % = 
DIVIDE ( 
    [Net Realized Profit], 
    [Total Sales INR], 
    0 
)

Safe division protecting against zero-sales scenarios, calculating accurate profit margin percentage.

[Sales SPLY (Prior Year)] Time Intelligence 
Sales SPLY = 
CALCULATE (
    [Total Sales INR],
    SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR ( 'Dim_Fiscal_Calendar'[Date] )
)

Shifts fiscal calendar context by 1 full year to establish comparative prior year benchmark.
