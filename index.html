DAX Formulas & Star Schema Architecture

Direct inspection of business measures, dimensional relationships, and Power Query ETL steps

[Total Sales INR] Core Revenue 
Total Sales INR = 
SUM ( 'Fact_Retail_Orders'[Gross_Sales_Amount] )

Calculates the total gross invoiced revenue in Indian Rupees across all filtered dimensions.

[Net Realized Profit] Profitability 
Net Realized Profit = 
SUM ( 'Fact_Retail_Orders'[Net_Profit_Amount] )

Computes realized net margin after deducting wholesale procurement cost, logistics, and promotional discounts.

[Blended Profit Margin %] Efficiency 
Blended Profit Margin % = 
DIVIDE ( 
    [Net Realized Profit], 
    [Total Sales INR], 
    0 
)

Handles divide-by-zero safely to return exact percentage margin across categories and zones.

[Sales SPLY (Prior Year)] Time Intelligence 
Sales SPLY = 
CALCULATE (
    [Total Sales INR],
    SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR ( 'Dim_Calendar'[Date] )
)

Evaluates sales for the identical calendar window in the prior financial year for accurate YoY benchmarking.

[YoY Sales Growth %] Time Intelligence 
YoY Sales Growth % = 
VAR CurrentSales = [Total Sales INR]
VAR PriorYearSales = [Sales SPLY]
RETURN
    DIVIDE ( CurrentSales - PriorYearSales, PriorYearSales, 0 )

Computes year-over-year revenue expansion percentage.

[GST Collected INR] Tax Compliance 
GST Collected INR = 
SUM ( 'Fact_Retail_Orders'[GST_Amount] )

Aggregates output GST liability (CGST + SGST / IGST) based on product HSN classification.

Step 1

Data Extraction & Encoding

Extracted raw sales transactions from Point of Sale (POS) and e-commerce databases. Applied UTF-8 encoding with rupee currency normalization.

Step 2

Data Cleaning & Deduplication

Removed cancelled orders, formatted Indian dates (DD-MM-YYYY), and converted negative profit outliers into standard audit flags.

Step 3

Data Privacy & Masking

Masked customer mobile numbers and PII using SHA-256 hashing to ensure data compliance and privacy best practices.

Step 4

Star Schema & Fiscal Hierarchy

Constructed an Indian financial calendar hierarchy (April to March fiscal year) linked to 1 Fact and 4 Dimension tables for ultra-fast query execution.
