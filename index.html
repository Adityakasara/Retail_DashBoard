Power BI DAX Measures & Data Model

[Total Sales INR]

Total Sales INR = 
SUM ( 'Fact_Retail_Orders'[Gross_Sales_Amount] )

Calculates top-line invoiced retail revenue in Indian Rupees.

[Net Realized Profit]

Net Realized Profit = 
SUM ( 'Fact_Retail_Orders'[Net_Profit_Amount] )

Net gross profit considering manufacturing cost and discounts.

[Profit Margin %]

Profit Margin % = 
DIVIDE ( [Net Realized Profit], [Total Sales INR], 0 )

Calculates profitability percentage with safe zero handling.

[Sales SPLY]

Sales SPLY = 
CALCULATE ( 
    [Total Sales INR], 
    SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR ( 'Dim_Fiscal_Calendar'[Date] ) 
)

Shifts fiscal calendar context by 1 full year for YoY benchmarking.
